# Public samples

`integrated_restaurants_sample.csv` contains ten real rows selected from the final
integrated table: four restaurants present on three platforms, three present on two,
and three present on one. Only fields needed to illustrate the integrated schema are
included.

The sample intentionally omits source URLs, contact data, review text, reviewer
profiles, and raw platform payloads. It is a structural example, not a benchmark or a
representative statistical sample.

Field definitions for the complete integrated dataset are documented in
[`services/transform/unified_dataset/integrated-dataset-schema.md`](../../services/transform/unified_dataset/integrated-dataset-schema.md).
See [`DATA_USAGE.md`](../../DATA_USAGE.md) for reuse conditions.
